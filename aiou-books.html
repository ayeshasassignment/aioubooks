Aam sawalat

Kya ye books waqai free hain?

Ji haan. Ye Allama Iqbal Open University ki official books hain jo university khud students ke liye free faraham karti hai. Download button aap ko seedha AIOU ke official server (online.aiou.edu.pk) par le jata hai.

Full aur Short version mein kya farq hai?

Full version poori kitab hai. Short version — jahan available ho — usi book ka mukhtasar version hai jo AIOU ne khaas taur par tayyar kiya hai.

Meri book list mein nahi mil rahi, kya karun?

Course code theek se check karein. Phir bhi na mile to WhatsApp (0314 0774267) par code bhejein — hum dhoond kar link bhej dein ge.
